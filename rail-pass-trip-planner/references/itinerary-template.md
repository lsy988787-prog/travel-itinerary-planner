# Itinerary document template

Write the final document in the user's language. Lead each section with its point (date, route, where you sleep), then the tables. Keep prose short; the tables carry the plan.

```markdown
---
title: <Trip name> (<start date>–<end date>)
---

<One paragraph: length, pass used, number of paid reservations and their total, nights in paid hotels and cost range, how the trip ends.>

# Overview

| ... | ... | ... | ... | €0 |
```
